Removal ≠ offsetting

Traditional offsets avoid future emissions. Carbon removal physically extracts CO₂ already in the atmosphere and stores it durably (100+ years). If you want to neutralize your own emissions, prioritize verified removal over avoidance.

Why it matters now

The IPCC is clear: even with deep emission cuts, carbon removal is required alongside reductions to reach net-zero. Buying today accelerates learning, drives costs down, and builds the capacity we'll need at scale.
