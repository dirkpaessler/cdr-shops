## Carbon Drawdown Initiative

Our mission is to speed up negative emissions.

In 2025, when we looked around to purchase certificates for 200 tons of CDR to offset the emissions from our own activities (scientific experiments, travel, etc.) we found that it is not easy to actually find vendors for small CDR purchases.

Read [our blog post about this](https://www.carbon-drawdown.de/blog/2025-8-21-everybody-agrees-we-need-more-cdr-buyers-but-did-you-ever-try-to-buy-certificates-yourself).

We decided that the world needs a directory of online shops that sell CDR in small volumes to individuals and/or small businesses.

If you know of a shop that we have not listed here, please [suggest it](/suggest/) or write to [info@carbon-drawdown.de](mailto:info@carbon-drawdown.de?subject=Shop%20Suggestion%20for%20the%20CDR%20Shop%20Directory).

To find out more about the Carbon Drawdown Initiative please [visit our website](https://www.carbon-drawdown.de).
