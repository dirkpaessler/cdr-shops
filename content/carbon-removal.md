### Removal ≠ offsetting

Traditional offsets avoid future emissions. Carbon removal physically extracts CO₂ already in the atmosphere and stores it permanently (100+ years).

### The science says it's essential

[The IPCC is clear](https://www.ipcc.ch): even with deep emission cuts, carbon removal is required alongside reductions to reach net-zero. Starting now helps build the capacity we'll need at scale.
